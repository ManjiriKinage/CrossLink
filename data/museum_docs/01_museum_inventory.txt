Artifact Record: RVM-1842
Last Updated: 01 August 2024

Artifact Name: Blue Sapphire of Maratha
Category: Historical gemstone
Estimated Date: Late 19th century
Estimated Insurance Value: INR 8.5 crore
Permanent Collection: Riverview Museum, Mumbai
Normal Storage Location: Secure Collection Vault, Room V-3
Curator Responsible: Ananya Rao

Description:
The Blue Sapphire of Maratha is a deep-blue oval sapphire mounted in a silver and gold ceremonial setting. The museum acquired the artifact in 1987 from the estate of collector Prakash Kulkarni.

The artifact was scheduled for public display during the museum's August 2024 historical-gemstone exhibition. Any movement outside the collection vault required authorization from the curator and a security officer.

Security Note:
Artifact RVM-1842 must remain under museum custody at all times during the exhibition. Removal from museum premises requires written authorization from the Director and security verification.
