RIVERVIEW MUSEUM - INVESTIGATION TIMELINE
Case Reference: RM-2024-0813

1987
Riverview Museum acquires the Blue Sapphire of Maratha from the estate of collector Prakash Kulkarni.

05 Aug 2024
Artifact RVM-1842 is transferred from Collection Vault V-3 to Exhibition Hall B.

09 Aug 2024
Curator Ananya Rao documents a private acquisition inquiry from collector Rohan Mehta.

10 Aug 2024
Rohan Mehta visits the museum.

11 Aug 2024
Temporary technical access request is submitted for security technician Dev Malhotra.

12 Aug 2024
Rohan Mehta visits the museum again.
Ananya Rao inspects Exhibition Hall B.
Dev Malhotra performs security-system maintenance and enters the service corridor/security control area.
Rahul Deshmukh's scheduled shift ends at approximately 10:00 PM.
At 11:35 PM, CCTV coverage of the service corridor and Exhibition Hall B stops.
At 11:42 PM, Rahul Deshmukh's access card SD-17 is recorded in the service corridor.
At 12:05 AM, CCTV recording resumes.

13 Aug 2024
Museum staff discover that RVM-1842 is missing.

14 Aug 2024
Security incident report is prepared.

20 Aug 2024
Police interview Rahul Deshmukh and begin reviewing access records.

05 Sep 2024
Heritage Crown Auctions publishes Lot 118, a blue sapphire whose description and visible mount markings resemble RVM-1842.

Current Status
The identity of the auction seller has not been independently confirmed. The available records establish several relevant connections but do not conclusively identify the person responsible for the disappearance.
