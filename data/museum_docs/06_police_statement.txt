POLICE INVESTIGATION - INTERVIEW SUMMARY
Case Reference: RM-2024-0813
Date: 20 August 2024

Interviewee: Rahul Deshmukh
Role: Senior Security Officer

Rahul stated that he did not remove the Blue Sapphire and did not authorize anyone to remove it.

He confirmed that Card SD-17 was assigned to him and normally remained in his possession during working hours. He stated that he left the museum shortly after his shift ended at 10:00 PM.

Investigators asked about Dev Malhotra.

Rahul stated that Dev had legitimate access to the security control room because he was repairing the CCTV system. Rahul said that Dev had asked questions about the museum's camera layout earlier that evening.

Investigators also reviewed an access-card request submitted on 11 August. The request was for temporary technical access for Dev Malhotra.

No evidence in this interview establishes that Dev removed the artifact.

The investigation remains open.
