HERITAGE CROWN AUCTIONS - SEPTEMBER 2024 CATALOG
Catalog Publication Date: 05 September 2024

LOT 118
Title: Rare 19th Century Blue Sapphire in Ceremonial Mount

Description:
A deep-blue oval sapphire set in a silver and gold ceremonial mount, believed to originate from western India.

Estimated Value: INR 7.5 - 9 crore

The catalog photograph shows a distinctive scratch along the lower-right edge of the silver mount.

Investigators later compared the catalog description with Riverview Museum's inventory record for artifact RVM-1842. The museum record describes a deep-blue oval sapphire in a silver and gold ceremonial setting and also records a small scratch near the lower-right edge of the mount.

Seller: Private Collection
Identity withheld pending verification.

Auction House: Heritage Crown Auctions
Location: Mumbai

The auction house states that the seller provided documentation claiming private ownership, but the provenance documents had not yet been independently verified when the catalog was published.
