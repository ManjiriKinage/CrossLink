RIVERVIEW MUSEUM - SECURITY INCIDENT REPORT
Incident Date: 12 - 13 August 2024
Prepared: 14 August 2024
Prepared by: Rahul Deshmukh

At approximately 11:35 PM on 12 August, the CCTV feed covering the service corridor and Exhibition Hall B stopped recording.

The security control system recorded an access-card event at 11:42 PM.

Card ID: SD-17
Assigned to: Rahul Deshmukh
Location: Service Corridor, Exhibition Hall B

Rahul Deshmukh states that he was at home from approximately 10:30 PM onward and did not use his card at 11:42 PM.

The maintenance log shows that Dev Malhotra, an external security-system technician, was present at the museum earlier that evening. His work order covered inspection of the CCTV control equipment.

At 12:05 AM, the CCTV feed resumed.

No forced entry was visible on the available camera footage.

The report recommends investigating the use of Card SD-17 and reviewing the activities of all personnel with access to the security control area.
