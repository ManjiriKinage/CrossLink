Subject: Visitor inquiry regarding Blue Sapphire of Maratha

The museum received another inquiry from Rohan Mehta regarding the Blue Sapphire of Maratha.

Rohan Mehta is a private collector who has previously visited the museum's gemstone exhibitions. During a visit in July, he asked whether RVM-1842 might ever be available for private acquisition.

I informed him that the artifact is part of the permanent collection and is not currently for sale.

I have also reminded staff that private negotiations involving collection artifacts are not permitted during the exhibition.

For the record, Rohan Mehta is expected to visit the exhibition on 10 August and has requested access to the gemstone gallery.
