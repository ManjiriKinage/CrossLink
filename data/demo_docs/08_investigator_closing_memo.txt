SPECIAL INVESTIGATION BRANCH — INTERNAL MEMORANDUM
CONFIDENTIAL — EYES ONLY
TO: Deputy Commissioner of Police, Crime Branch, Mumbai
FROM: Inspector Kulkarni, Senior Investigating Officer
DATE: August 14, 1995
SUBJECT: Unsolved Cold Case Inquiry — Downtown Warehouse Arson & Financial Fraud

CASE OVERVIEW & SUSPECT TIMELINE:
Over the past sixty days, investigators reviewed physical evidence, corporate registries, and eyewitness accounts surrounding the catastrophic fire at Downtown Warehouse on June 14, 1995.

KEY FINDINGS & CROSS-EVIDENCE CORRELATION:

1. MOTIVE & CONSPIRACY:
Mehta Industries faced an impending inspection by federal revenue auditors on June 16, 1995, regarding offshore fund diversions through Apex Shipping Co.
Managing Director John Mehta orchestrated the destruction of physical transaction books housed in Downtown Warehouse.

2. LOGISTICAL ACCOMPLICES:
Sarah Rao, as Logistics Director, cleared the evacuation of high-value contraband and disabled facility security lights under instruction.
Her claim of early departure is contradicted by security logs from Vikram Joshi, placing her near loading bay 3 until after 22:30 hours.

3. PHYSICAL EVIDENCE TRACE:
The discovery of John Mehta's inscribed pocket watch and master keys at the rear exit definitively establishes his physical presence at the Downtown Warehouse during the arson setup.

STATUS:
Arrest warrants prepared for John Mehta and Sarah Rao.
However, John Mehta fled Mumbai aboard an Apex Shipping cargo vessel toward international waters on July 10, 1995.
Sarah Rao remains under protective surveillance in Bandra.
Case designated pending apprehension. Master file archived at Colaba Police Archives.
