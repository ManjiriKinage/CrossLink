TRANSCRIPT OF POLICE INTERROGATION
INVESTIGATION BRANCH, CRIME CELL
DATE: June 19, 1995
SUBJECT: Sarah Rao, Logistics Director, Downtown Warehouse
LOCATION: Colaba Police Station, Mumbai
INTERROGATOR: Inspector Kulkarni

[TRANSCRIPT START]

INSPECTOR KULKARNI: State your full name and occupation for the record.
SARAH RAO: Sarah Rao. Director of Freight Operations and Logistics at the Downtown Warehouse.

INSPECTOR KULKARNI: You were at the warehouse premises on June 14, 1995. Can you explain your whereabouts between 20:00 and 23:45 hours?
SARAH RAO: I was preparing shipment logs. We had received an urgent consignment dispatch order from Mehta Industries.

INSPECTOR KULKARNI: Did you meet with John Mehta that afternoon?
SARAH RAO: Yes, John Mehta came to my office at 16:30 hours. He informed me that government tax auditors from the Ministry were planning a raid on the warehouse on June 16. He demanded that certain crate batches marked 'Apex Shipping' be cleared out immediately.

INSPECTOR KULKARNI: Vikram Joshi claims you ordered the security lights extinguished at 21:00 hours.
SARAH RAO: John Mehta ordered that, not me! He said the transfer had to remain discrete. I met an intermediary driver from Bandra Docks at 22:15 hours to transfer 40 containers of chemical solvents.

INSPECTOR KULKARNI: If you were simply moving cargo, why did the building explode at 23:45 hours?
SARAH RAO: I left at 22:45 hours. I was driving toward Nariman Point when I saw the smoke plume. John Mehta had a spare key to the rear emergency vault. I believe he sabotaged the facility to destroy the audit trail before inspectors could examine the books.

[TRANSCRIPT CONCLUDED]
