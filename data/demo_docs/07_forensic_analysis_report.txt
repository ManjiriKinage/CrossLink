FORENSIC SCIENCE LABORATORY
STATE POLICE HEADQUARTERS, MUMBAI
CASE FILE: ARSON / CR-1995-0842 (DOWNTOWN WAREHOUSE)
DATE OF REPORT: July 02, 1995
CHIEF EXAMINER: Dr. H. S. Bakshi

EXAMINATION OF PHYSICAL EVIDENCE:

ITEM A-1: Charred Timber and Concrete Swabs from Bay 3
Gas chromatography revealed high concentrations of industrial grade Benzene and Xylene accelerant.
Pattern analysis of burn marks confirms that two distinct ignition sources were simultaneously triggered using an electronic timer mechanism.

ITEM A-2: Salvaged Metal Safe from Admin Office 4
The vault door had been opened via correct mechanical combination prior to fire ignition.
Financial ledgers spanning 1991–1995 were deliberately removed; remaining paper remnants consisted of discarded packing slips.

ITEM A-3: Personal Effects Recovered from Rear Emergency Exit
Found lodged beneath the scorched loading ramp:
1. One customized gold pocket watch with inscription: "To John Mehta, Chairman - Mehta Industries, 1990".
2. Set of brass keys corresponding to the master deadbolt of the Downtown Warehouse rear gate.

CONCLUSION:
The fire at Downtown Warehouse was premeditated arson executed with insider access to vault combinations and keys.
Accelerant composition directly matches solvents cataloged in dispatch manifests authorized by Sarah Rao on June 14, 1995.
