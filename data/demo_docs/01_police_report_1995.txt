CASE INCIDENT REPORT
COLABA POLICE DIVISION, MUMBAI
INCIDENT ID: CR-1995-0842
DATE OF REPORT: June 15, 1995
OFFICER: Inspector Kulkarni

SUMMARY OF INCIDENT:
On the night of June 14, 1995, at approximately 23:45 hours, patrol units were dispatched following reports of an intense fire and structural explosion at the Downtown Warehouse located near the old docks in Mumbai.

Upon arrival, fire tenders from the Bombay Municipal Corporation were engaged in containment operations. Initial perimeter assessment indicated that the warehouse gates had been unlocked prior to the blaze.

PRELIMINARY WITNESS FINDINGS:
Officers questioned Sarah Rao, who serves as the Logistics Director for the facility. Sarah Rao stated that she had left the Downtown Warehouse at 20:30 hours after completing routine inventory audits. However, security personnel contradicted this timeline, stating that Sarah Rao was observed speaking with an unidentified male near loading dock B shortly before 22:15 hours.

Records obtained from the gate register document that John Mehta, managing director of Mehta Industries, entered the Downtown Warehouse premises earlier that afternoon on June 14, 1995. When questioned informally, John Mehta denied having any business at the facility after 17:00 hours.

PHYSICAL EVIDENCE COLLECTED:
1. Residue samples containing chemical accelerants retrieved from loading bay 3.
2. Severed padlock from the rear emergency exit.
3. Handwritten shipping manifest bearing the signature of Sarah Rao.

Case transferred to Special Investigation Branch for further inquiry.
