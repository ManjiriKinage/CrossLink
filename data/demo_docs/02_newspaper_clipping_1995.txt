THE BOMBAY CHRONICLE
CITY EDITION — June 16, 1995

MIDNIGHT INFERNO ENGULFS DOWNTOWN WAREHOUSE: SUSPECTED ARSON AT OLD DOCKS

MUMBAI — An inferno consumed the historic Downtown Warehouse late Wednesday night, destroying goods estimated in the tens of millions of rupees. Local authorities believe the blaze was intentionally set using industrial accelerants.

The facility, which served as the primary transit depot for Mehta Industries and affiliated logistics partners, was undergoing a confidential internal audit. Financial circles in Nariman Point were already shaken earlier this month following rumors of severe accounting irregularities within Mehta Industries.

Witnesses reported seeing a dark luxury sedan matching the vehicle owned by tycoon John Mehta fleeing the dock road minutes before the sirens sounded. While John Mehta has refused to comment publicly, his legal representatives issued a statement asserting that he spent the entirety of June 14, 1995, at his Nariman Point residence.

Meanwhile, investigators have sought Sarah Rao, the facility's chief logistics manager, for emergency questioning. Inspector Kulkarni of the Colaba Police confirmed that records recovered from the site point to unauthorized late-night cargo transfers between Downtown Warehouse and vessels bound for foreign ports.
