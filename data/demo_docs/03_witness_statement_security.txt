RECORD OF WITNESS INTERVIEW
COLABA POLICE DEPARTMENT
WITNESS: Vikram Joshi (Age 42)
OCCUPATION: Head Gate Security Guard, Downtown Warehouse
DATE: June 17, 1995
INTERVIEWING OFFICER: Inspector Kulkarni

STATEMENT:
"I have been employed as the nighttime security supervisor at the Downtown Warehouse for six years. On Wednesday, June 14, 1995, my shift commenced at 18:00 hours.

Around 16:30 hours during the previous shift, John Mehta arrived in his personal vehicle and proceeded directly to the administrative wing to meet with Sarah Rao. Mr. Mehta appeared agitated. I saw them arguing outside office 4 near the inner courtyard.

Later that evening, around 21:00 hours, Ms. Sarah Rao instructed me to disable the automated security lights along loading bay 3, claiming that electrical maintenance was underway. I hesitated, but she insisted she had direct authorization from Mehta Industries headquarters in Nariman Point.

At 22:15 hours, an unmarked flatbed truck arrived at the gate. Sarah Rao personally signed the gate entry sheet and escorted the vehicle into the depot. I never saw that truck leave through the main gate.

At 23:30 hours, I smelled burning petroleum near loading bay 3. As I ran toward the rear yard, I heard a loud detonation and saw flames erupting from the storage racks. I immediately contacted the fire brigade and the Colaba Police Station."
